Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about planning your journey with us.

How does RoutheonSkups create my trip plan?
expand_more
It uses your destination, dates, budget, and interests to generate a day-wise itinerary with route flow, suggested attractions, and practical pacing.
Can I edit, save, and revisit my trips later?
expand_more
Yes. You can save your generated plan to your account and come back anytime to update trip details as your schedule changes.
What kind of notifications will I receive?
expand_more
You can receive trip countdown alerts, AI travel suggestions, seasonal recommendations, and system updates. All notification types can be managed from Settings.
Does it include weather and seasonal guidance?
expand_more
Yes. Destination weather support and season-based recommendations help you pick better travel dates and plan each day more comfortably.
Is my account and trip data private?
expand_more
Your saved trips, profile preferences, and itinerary data stay linked to your account and are used to personalize your planning experience.
