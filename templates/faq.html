Getting Started

RoutheonSkups is an AI-powered travel planning platform that helps you discover destinations, create personalized itineraries, and plan perfect trips. It combines Groq AI technology with a database of 800+ destinations across 40+ countries, along with weather data, route optimization, and smart notifications — all in one sleek, dark-themed interface.

Click the Register button and sign up using your name, email, and password. You can also use Google Sign-In from the login page for a faster, one-click sign-up experience. Once registered, you'll have full access to all platform features including trip planning, AI chat, favorites, and more.

Yes! RoutheonSkups is completely free to use. All core features — including AI-powered trip planning, destination exploration, Skupheon AI chat, favorites, notifications, and weather updates — are available at no cost. There are no hidden fees, subscription plans, or premium tiers.

You can view public pages like the landing page without logging in, but to access the full experience — including FAQ, trip planning, saved destinations, Skupheon AI, profile management, notifications, and favorites — you'll need to create an account and log in. This ensures your data and plans are saved securely.

RoutheonSkups works on any modern web browser — Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge, and more. The platform is fully responsive and works beautifully on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. No app download is required; just visit the website and start planning.

Trip Planning

The trip planner is a guided 4-step wizard. Step 1: Choose your destination. Step 2: Select travel dates, group size, and budget. Step 3: Pick your interests and preferences. Step 4: AI generates a personalized day-by-day itinerary with activities, restaurants, and hidden gems. You can review and save your plan to access anytime from My Trips.

Yes! The AI Prompt Trip Planner lets you describe your dream trip in natural language — for example, "Plan a 7-day budget trip to Japan with my partner in spring" — and the AI will create a detailed itinerary based on your description. It's a faster, more conversational alternative to the step-by-step wizard.

Absolutely. All created trips are stored in your account and visible in My Trips on your profile. You can open any saved trip to view the full itinerary, check details, and reference it whenever you need. Trips are organized so you can see upcoming and past adventures at a glance.

RoutheonSkups uses Dijkstra's algorithm and Traveling Salesman Problem (TSP) optimization to calculate the most efficient routes between destinations in your itinerary. This means your daily plans are optimized for minimal travel time and distance, so you spend more time exploring and less time commuting.

Yes, you can create as many trips as you want. Plan a weekend getaway, a month-long adventure, or multiple trips for different seasons — there's no limit. All your trips are saved in your My Trips dashboard for easy access.

Currently, you can view your complete saved itinerary from My Trips. To make changes, you can create a new trip with updated preferences and let the AI generate a fresh itinerary based on your revised plans.

AI Assistant — Skupheon

Skupheon is RoutheonSkups' AI travel assistant, powered by Groq's Llama 3.3 70B Versatile model. It's a ChatGPT-style workspace where you can have natural conversations about travel. Ask for destination recommendations, trip advice, local tips, or general travel questions — Skupheon provides intelligent, contextual responses.

You can ask Skupheon almost anything travel-related: "What are the best beaches in Thailand?", "Plan a 5-day trip to Paris on a budget", "What should I pack for Iceland in winter?", "Compare Bali vs Phuket for a family vacation", and much more. It can also analyze travel images you share and provide information about landmarks or places.

The workspace features session management (create, switch between, and delete chat sessions), pinned conversations, file and image attachments, voice input, text-to-speech output, and a My Trip context selector that lets Skupheon reference your saved trips for more relevant advice. It's a complete AI travel companion.

Yes! Within a chat session, Skupheon maintains conversation context and remembers your previous messages. This means you can have back-and-forth conversations where the AI builds on what you discussed earlier — just like talking to a real travel agent who remembers your preferences.

Yes! The Skupheon workspace supports voice input through your browser's microphone. You can speak your travel questions instead of typing them. Skupheon also has text-to-speech capability, so it can read its responses aloud with multiple voice options — perfect for hands-free use while multitasking.

Yes! Skupheon uses the Llama 3.2 11B Vision model to analyze images. Upload a photo of a landmark, restaurant, scenic view, or any travel-related image and the AI can identify it, provide information, and give travel tips related to what you've shared.

In the Skupheon workspace, you can select one of your saved trips as context. This allows the AI to reference your specific trip details — destination, dates, itinerary — when answering questions. For example, if you ask "What should I do on day 3?", Skupheon will know exactly which trip and itinerary you're referring to.

Yes! Each destination page has its own AI chat assistant that specializes in that specific location. Ask about the best time to visit, local cuisine, must-see spots, transportation tips, or any destination-specific questions and get instant, relevant answers.

Destinations & Explore

RoutheonSkups covers 800+ popular destinations across 40+ countries spanning Asia, Europe, the Americas, Africa, and Oceania. Each destination includes detailed information about attractions, local tips, curated itineraries, image galleries, and real-time weather data.

The Atlas Console is an interactive exploration interface. It features cascading Country → Region → City dropdowns, 42 category tiles (Beaches, Mountains, Historical Sites, Nightlife, Food & Dining, Adventure, and many more), and AI-powered destination discovery with smart pagination. Simply select a country and region to browse all available destinations.

Each destination page includes a hero banner, curated attractions with descriptions, image galleries, detailed travel itineraries, real-time weather data, AI-powered destination chat support, save-to-favorites functionality, and local travel tips. It's a complete guide for that location.

The platform offers 42 curated travel categories including: Beaches, Mountains, Historical Sites, Religious Places, Nightlife, Food & Dining, Adventure, Wildlife, Photography, Shopping, Museums, Art & Culture, Hot Springs, Lakes, Islands, Waterfalls, Caves, National Parks, Temples, Castles, and many more. You can filter destinations by any of these categories.

Weather data is available on destination pages and in your trip details. The platform uses the Open-Meteo API to provide real-time weather conditions and forecasts. You can check temperature, humidity, wind, and conditions before you travel so you know exactly what to pack and expect.

Yes! The Explore page has a search bar that lets you search destinations by name. You can also use the cascading dropdowns to filter by country, region, and city. Additionally, the category tiles let you discover destinations by travel interest.

Privacy & Security

Your data is protected using industry-standard security measures. Passwords are encrypted with Flask-Bcrypt before storage. The platform uses Flask-Login for secure session management, CSRF protection on forms, and input validation to prevent common vulnerabilities. Your data is never shared with third parties.

No. RoutheonSkups does not sell, share, or distribute your personal data to third parties. Your profile information, trip plans, favorites, and chat history are stored securely in your personal account and are only accessible by you.

Yes. Google OAuth uses industry-standard OAuth 2.0 protocol. When you sign in with Google, your password is never shared with RoutheonSkups. Google handles the authentication, and we receive only the minimal profile information needed to create your account.

Password reset uses time-limited, unique tokens sent to your registered email. The reset link expires after 30 minutes to prevent unauthorized access. If you didn't request a reset, simply ignore the email — no changes will be made to your account.

If you choose to delete your account, all your personal data including profile information, trip plans, favorites, chat sessions, and notifications are permanently removed from our servers. This action is irreversible.

Technical Support

Try these troubleshooting steps: 1) Clear your browser cache and cookies, 2) Disable browser extensions temporarily, 3) Try a different browser or incognito mode, 4) Check your internet connection, 5) Update your browser to the latest version. If issues persist, contact our technical support with details about your browser and device.

If Skupheon isn't responding, try: 1) Refresh the page, 2) Check your internet connection, 3) Try sending a shorter or different message, 4) Clear your browser cache. The AI service is generally very reliable, but occasional delays may occur due to high demand. If the problem persists, contact support.

Use the Contact page to send us a message. Include details about your issue, your browser/device info, and any error messages you've seen. The system also sends a confirmation email to your registered address so you know we've received your request.

Voice input requires browser microphone permission. Make sure: 1) Your browser supports the Web Speech API (Chrome works best), 2) You've granted microphone permission to the site, 3) Your microphone hardware is working, 4) You're not in a tab that's been backgrounded. Try refreshing the page and re-granting microphone access.

Make sure your image is in a supported format (JPG, PNG, or WebP) and is a reasonable file size. A slow internet connection may cause upload delays. Try compressing the image or using a different format. If the issue persists, try refreshing the page and uploading again.

Absolutely! RoutheonSkups is fully responsive and works great on mobile devices. Simply open the website in your mobile browser. The interface automatically adapts to your screen size with a mobile-friendly navigation menu, touch-optimized buttons, and layouts designed for smaller screens.
